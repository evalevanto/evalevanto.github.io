Surfacing like a whale, spluttering desperately - about time for that gasp of air.

This moment here, is captured in the beginning of a week that ended about 1/2 a mile longer.

What is insomnia and the ghosts that stay up dancing with you!

You are the blur of many streetlights that shout in the emptiness of a highway in the deep of the night.

You are the groggy morning voice scratching throats like metal spoons screeching on metal plates.

Bones cracking under the weight of a cracking brain.

But alas, the ending week and the illusion of finality and newness. Give this tired running soul some sleep; in velvety clouds, cotton candy - the fluffiness of a pillow I'd want.

I am the poetry of Sisyphus - the whistles in the plunges, whales leaping like flowers blooming in the light.
