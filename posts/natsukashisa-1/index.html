it was my little space, and i loved it.

18 Jan 2026 · story

it was my little space, and i loved it. i hate that i never took pictures, and if i did, then that i didn’t take enough.

i’d wake up around 6:30 to the sun warm on my face, kissing me on my mattress on the floor. i slept on the floor. i’d roll over, get up and walk to the balcony door, open it and stretch.

the beige tiles in my kitchen would be gleaming by then. i loved to read there. i had a three-legged swahili stool that i turned into a makeshift seat with a borrowed couch pillow.

i cannot remember a single book i read there like how i remember the first book i ever bought: The Zanzibar Chest. i clutched it randomly at a store at the end of a street in a quiet side of town the day before i boarded a plane for the first time, leaving my home for the first time, flying to Morocco to see a man i’d only known for two weeks.
