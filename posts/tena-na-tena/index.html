Tena na Tena | Mwanzo

The universe and a handful of humans know how many times I have started afresh.

25 May 2025 · essay

The universe and a handful of humans know how many times I have started afresh.

Just yesterday, I gave a whole new fresh organization to my Todoist - let’s rename these projects… remove the emojis from the titles this time around… switch to the calendar view 😍. It’s that personal website, proudly hosted on GitHub, that gets a cool theme revamp every new year and then left for the tumbleweeds.

I am not sure why I do it. This self-deception — stylized stagnation kept alive off-kiltered, like a stalling car, by inertia’s momentary intense jerks. Inertia.

“Ah, a jumpstart is what I need - let me smell that hot oil burning in the engine, and I’ll be off to Timbuktu1 in no time!” But you don’t like to drive, and when you do, it’s Jesus take the wheel2 how you get to your destination.

So here I am, a fresh coat of paint, a new blog and a delusional spirit with paint brushes: Mwanzo, tena and tena!

1

You know, the everywhere Timbuktu?

2

How did we even get there? You’re technically behind the wheel but spiritually in the passenger seat, some divine cruise control gets you there in one piece.
