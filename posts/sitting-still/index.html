S.36

Sitting Still

Prologue

17 Jun 2025 · story

Prologue

On a sunny afternoon, in your usual panic caused by your own chaos, with your half packed bag and a rapidly evaporating mental note of soon to be forgotten items; being already half an hour behind schedule, you just realize it's a 2-hr trip you are about to take. The irony of this moment and the expectation of calmness on that same evening is very surreal to you.

Your brown teenage tabby cat is zooming all around the room oblivious of your departure, and you hug your partner goodbye and walk to your cab.

Like a drum, the way it's incessantly beating as you try to explain to your brother where you are going. As you try to ask him to let your parents carefully into the news - something you didn't have the courage to do yourself. Then, the dark sky erupts in fat raindrops falling like hailstones on a windscreen - "I miss you" said through cracked voices on a phone call. You learn that your partner is sad.
You left.
You've never been away from each other recently. You are not going far but you are going AWOL. Oh, bless the cab driver for the Christian worship songs he had on during the ride - because nothing could have explained the round of tissues wet with salt, ripe eyes and an anchored tongue.

And finally you arrive. It is the middle of nowhere. It’s quiet. You are unsure what face to make as you follow a frayed yellow rope tied to metal column beams guiding you like a hesitant promise to the registration room.
How big a smile?
Should you smile at all? Because here you have at last arrived. For 10 days of silence. For 10 vacation days you have opted to walk into Lukenya Hills for Vipassana. Surely, you should have a face. On top of the doubt, you wear a smile.

Minutes have never seemed so precious grasping your phone.1

4:30 p.m. - You compose yourself and casually lie to your mother you are stretching your legs outside the house shopping groceries for supper. 4:40 p.m. - You call your partner... No answer.4:42 p.m. - You call your partner... No answer. Damn! You tell your brother goodbye.4:55 p.m. - Your partner finally calls back - you crackle the words that tell your partner goodbye.
“Send me pictures everyday even if I won’t see them.”
“…”
“I’ll miss you.”5:00 p.m. - You hand over your phone.


You are Theodore in Her, alone in an empty apartment as Samantha’s final words fade into the silence.

Disclaimer: Some events occurred. Some were just felt strongly.

I.

A girl sits fidgeting on a blue cushion that is about 6 inches above the ground getting ready to focus on her breathe for an hour. She’s never done this before. Every time she notices the waft through her nostrils, she pants - as if she’s chasing the air, trying to trap it long enough to observe it.

“How long do you go away for?”

The moment a girl clutches her shins and closes her eyes - she’s Icarus breaking out, flying back to her cat.

“Alright — when you notice that you have been away, just bring yourself back.”

Look at her wings melting as she plummets back to the ground. With no harshness, she straightens her back to begin again.

She knows when its 6 am because the morning chants yank her alert in the cold meditation hall. Its 2 hours since the bell that pulled her out of REM sleep like getting out of a waterboarding round — she's been up since 4am.
Goenka’s2 voice ripples through — vibrating against the walls of her chest like massive waves crashing against a rocky shore. Breathe.
And she watches it, with nothing else to do — this infinite tide. Rising. Falling.
Breathe.

Interlude

Everything is quiet —
and the mind has never been louder.
It’s less murky, less muddled —
so it’s more pronounced.
A song that never quits.
A voice that is always noting.
You cannot silence it —
it will be like stirring a boiling pot.

You acknowledge it.
Then you learn to mute it —
to ignore it.

II.

I channel Neferpitou’s En3.
I am past day 4 — now learning to sit still and scan every inch of my body from the tip of my head to my toes for sensations.
Me and my sinuses are barely surviving these sittings. The flies are having a blast testing my patience and mocking when I flinch. I decided to sit cross-legged for the session and now my whole body is trembling like trying to hold my breath for 2 minutes.

I have to experience the truth for myself —

About 30 minutes in, as I am scanning the upper body, like gravity, my mind is getting constantly pulled to the pain in my legs. I remind yourself — everything passes. Focus. Maintain the rhythm.

Then I reach my thigh and a flood of pain pours in - fresh and intense. I feel heat that is quickly radiating outwards to my whole being. I focus intently on this sensation. There is pressure at the knees and a tightness in my muscles. I keep scanning and now throbbing — like little heartbeats underneath the (labelled) "pain". Then suddenly, it’s not pain anymore. Just uncomfortable tingling - like needles dancing on my skin. Then even that fades. I am well past your limbs now, working my way up the body.
There’s only pulsations.

My mind is blown.

Everything changes. Constantly. Endlessly. It is useless to react to what is impermanent.

I still get up to go cry outside and talk to birds.

III.

Stop generating new Sankara and automatically the old Sankara will come to the surface.

You had expectations:
It’s day 8 — where are the tears? Where is the long forgotten memory from 20 years ago that you should be healing from now?

So for a couple of days, you brought along with you a Sankara generator.
Trying to not react made you so anxious you could not stop reacting.
You tried to not feel fear when the lights went out — even after you’d seen a scorpion earlier, just outside your bedroom door. You did not let yourself enjoy the tea after adding masala.
You tried to control what was meant to be observed objectively.

You started flinching whenever you “did not feel any sensation” and lingered a bit too long whenever you experienced pulsations. You were gripping tightly what was supposed to pass through open hands.

But the instruction was simple:

With a calm and quiet mind. Aware and attentive mind. Remain equanimous with the understanding that everything changes.

During a discourse, you learnt about Buddha, the scientist — the one who sat under the Bodhi tree to observe, to study the mind by watching it. You too, could distance yourself from your mind and observe it curiously.
You battled a back pain all through that night and into the next day. When it completely dissolved in the middle of a session, you got up to go cry outside and talk to birds.

Epilogue

Then came the end of the Silence,
Like delayed thunder after lightning.

2

S.N. Goenka (1924–2013) was a renowned teacher of Vipassana meditation in the tradition of Sayagyi U Ba Khin. His audio discourses and chants guide students during 10-day silent retreats around the world.

3

Neferpitou is a terrifying and adorable chimera ant from the anime/manga Hunter x Hunter. Their signature ability, “En,” lets them extend their aura in all directions to detect even the tiniest movement in a massive radius—think psychic spider-sense dialed to 11.
