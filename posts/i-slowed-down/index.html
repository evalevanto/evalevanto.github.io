S.34

I slowed down today.

2024 Dec 09

25 May 2025 · poem

This is a reflection of a day, after what had been a 3-month dash through the rainy Aberdare Ranges, and dreamy walks in the high altitude Teleki Valley.

On this sunny and quiet Monday, I hiked slowly. I sat and collapsed my tired muscles under the Ngong tree and breathed in the cold air.

What did you find,
when you stopped and looked?
When you walked along that smooth surface -
did you find the rough edges?
The steep hills and nasty nasty valleys you had to plunge and ascend?

Did you find two frothing souls -
hungry and hurt,
starving each other,
they've been thirsty for years! 
Did you water the dying plants?

Did you decide their flowers too wilted to revive?

What did you see,
when you peeked behind the rainbow?
We're you astonished,
at the gaping holes that riddled the canvas?
Did you see only a white light refracted by teardrops - 
a tragic beauty sustained by pain?
 
When you stopped and looked,
what did you think?
Did you feel your smallness?
Did it numb you in shock?

Your uselessness? 

Did you think yourself blissfully lost,
did you close your eyes and dance with the wind?

Did you think yourself the wind? 

When you finally stopped and looked,
what did you tell yourself?

Did you love yourself as your words disappeared into the wind?

Did you scream yourself dumb?
Did you whisper at the tree and let it hug you?

When you stopped,
did you let yourself stop?
